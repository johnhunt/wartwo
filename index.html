How to Play

Mine gold and cut lumber with your workers, build farms for food, raise an army at the barracks and destroy every enemy building and unit.

Left click select · drag box-select (max 9) · Shift add to selection · double click select all of a type on screen
Right click smart order: move, attack, harvest a mine or tree, return goods, set a rally point
A attack / attack-move · M move · S stop · H stand ground · G harvest · R return goods
B basic buildings · V advanced buildings · building hotkeys are shown on the buttons
Ctrl+1–9 make a group, 1–9 recall it (press twice to centre)
Arrow keys or the screen edge scroll · click the minimap to jump · Space jump to the last alert · Enter chat · F10 menu · P pause (single player)

Tech: Lumber Mill → archers, towers and the Forge · Barracks → Stables → cavalry · Forge → siege engines.
